How long it takes

Every Move states its own effort, and every dependency points at an earlier Move, so this is calculated rather than drawn by hand: the critical path is 160 weeks, and with 3 engineers the whole book is about 242. None of it is a promise — effort figures are estimates, and the constraint is usually the overlap window rather than the calendar.
