What it costs

$24,000 a month becomes $11,151 with the salary counted. That is $154,184 a year, or 54 per cent of the bill.

The reference build is 5 machines and a spare on the shelf, in one cage. Every figure below is a public list price observed while writing, against a bill this size. Substitute your own and the shape does not change.

Before you sign anything

Almost nobody should sign a facility contract before they have run this stack once. 3 refurbished machines on a managed switch under a desk cost about $1,840 once and $30 a month in electricity, and they run the whole of Stage 3 unchanged. It saves nothing, and that is not what it is for: it is the smallest bet that tells you whether the rest of this book is for you.

Every figure on this page is a dated list-price observation, not a quotation. Cloud prices are on-demand and undiscounted on purpose; a reader with a commitment discount should substitute their own effective rate, and a reader without one is genuinely paying this.
