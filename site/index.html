How to use this

  1. Find where you are. Pick the sentence below that sounds like your estate. It takes you to the stage you start at.
  2. Work down the checklist in order. Each Move says what has to be finished first, what it costs, how long the cutover is and how to undo it. Open one and follow it.
  3. Tick them off. This page remembers, in this browser, and tells you what is next.

Back to Metal

Every Move states its cutover in minutes of user-visible downtime, its risk as blast radius, and how long the thing it replaces must stay warm before you turn it off. The schedule is computed from the Moves’ own effort figures and the dependency graph, not asserted. Prices are public list rates observed while writing and will drift. Read the rollback page before running anything.
