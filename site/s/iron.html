Every Move states its cutover in minutes of user-visible downtime, its risk as blast radius, and how long the thing it replaces must stay warm before you turn it off. The schedule is computed from the Moves’ own effort figures and the dependency graph, not asserted. Prices are public list rates observed while writing and will drift. Read the rollback page before running anything.
